Meet the team.

A small, warm founding team of instructors, hand-picked for craft and character. Meet the faces — and the people — you'll see behind the microphone at flowhouse.

founder Sam

Sam is the founder of flowhouse and a 500-hour Comprehensive Pilates Instructor with additional training in yoga, barre, and integrative health coaching. Her approach to movement blends Pilates, strength, and control with a focus on creating classes that feel challenging, intentional, and approachable. Outside the studio, she loves being by the ocean, reading, and spending time with friends and family.

@samghabraa Spotify
instructor Alexis

Alexis is a registered nurse and FlowFormer instructor with a deep appreciation for the human body and the way it moves. Her classes focus on intentional movement, mind-muscle connection, and creating a supportive space where clients feel comfortable being challenged. Her goal is for you to leave feeling strong, accomplished, and excited to come back.

@Alexisporohnavi Spotify
instructor Bladen Potter

With a decade of teaching experience and a degree in Applied Physiology & Kinesiology and Health Education from the University of Florida, Bladen brings both science and soul to every flowhouse class. Living with Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome — a connective tissue disorder that's meant a lifetime of pain and numerous surgeries — she discovered firsthand that movement isn't just a practice, it's a lifeline, and that experience drives her focus on precise alignment, anatomical safety, and lasting strength in body and mind. She led flowhouse's instructor anatomy training, and her classes carry that same intention: movement with purpose, and you'll feel the difference.

want to teach at flowhouse?

Certification required. Movement obsession too.

You must be a certified Lagree, Xformer, Pilates, or barre instructor. From there, we train you in the Flowhouse Method in-house — with mentorship, on real reformers, in front of real classes.

what we look for

A certification, plus a few things we love.

  • Required: a current certification in Lagree, Xformer, Pilates, or barre
  • A pilates, barre, Lagree, Xformer, dance, or rehab background
  • Group-class teaching experience — any modality
  • Comfort with hands-on adjustments and clear verbal cueing
  • A point of view about programming you can defend in conversation
  • Above all: a calm, deep love for the practice
we'll train you in

The Flowhouse Method.

  • In-house training on the Flowformer method and machine
  • One-on-one mentorship through your first 90 days
  • Programming workshops every quarter
  • A real audition — not a résumé interview
  • A founding-team seat in shaping the practice
what we offer

A teaching environment built by people who teach.

Most studios optimise for the operator. We optimise for the instructor — because if the room behind the microphone is calm, the room in front of it is too.

Complimentary membership

Unlimited reformer + barre classes on our schedule for the duration of your time on the team. Movement keeps you sharp.

Discounted merchandise

Generous discount on the flowhouse apparel line. Wear it to teach, live in it after.

Thoughtful compensation

Competitive compensation, mentorship, and long-term growth opportunities within an expanding studio environment.

In-house training & mentorship

A real apprenticeship in the Flowhouse Method, one-on-one mentorship through your first 90 days, programming workshops every quarter.

Growth with the studio

Founding-team members shape what flowhouse becomes — the programming, the apprenticeship pipeline, the future locations. There's room to grow with us.

A community-focused environment

Small, warm, calm. We hire for warmth and craft, in that order. We protect the room — for our members and our team.
