the private experience

a little more than a workout.

Every private event is built around your group. Here's what it can include.

  1. 01A private 50-minute Reformer class or 45-minute Barre class
  2. 02A customized playlist and class atmosphere
  3. 03An experienced flowhouse instructor
  4. 04Private studio time afterward for pictures, cake, gifts, and celebrations
  5. 05The option to bring snacks, drinks, cakes, and decorations
  6. 06Optional flowhouse goodie bags featuring grip socks and other goodies

Additional details and personalized touches can be discussed when booking.

good to know

questions, answered.

Can we decorate the studio?

Absolutely! You're welcome to bring decorations. We'll coordinate setup details before your event.

Can we bring food and drinks?

Yes! You're welcome to bring cake, snacks, and beverages for your celebration.

What if some guests have never done Pilates or Barre?

No experience necessary. Our instructors can offer modifications and guide everyone through the experience.

Can we choose the music?

Of course. We want the experience to feel like yours.

How much does a private event cost?

Pricing depends on your group size and event details. Submit an inquiry for personalized information.

How do we reserve a date?

Complete the inquiry form below and our team will reach out to discuss availability and next steps.
