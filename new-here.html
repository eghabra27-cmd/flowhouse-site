new here

Not another pilates studio. A method of our own.

The Flowhouse experience blends contemporary Pilates with modern strength training on the FlowFormer — our custom reformer. Slow tempo, time under tension, breath, precision, and control, sequenced into one continuous flow.

Within one of our signature 50-minute classes, you can expect:

  • Breath work and deep-core activation on the FlowFormer — we start the conversation from the inside out.
  • Lower body, oblique, and upper body blocks — time-under-tension reps, slow eccentrics, and long-lever arms on the cables.
  • One continuous, breath-led flow. Your instructor guides every movement, transition, and spring change, and modifications are always available.
  • A final core push, then a slow stretch and cool down — you'll walk out taller than you came in.

First time on the reformer? Start with an Intro class — paced and cued for first-time movers, the place to learn the reformer before anything else.

First-timers should arrive about ten minutes before class for a short orientation and FlowFormer setup — your instructor will walk you through the carriage, platform, springs, straps, and safety features.

questions, answered

The most common questions from new clients.

Can't find your question? Email info@flowhouserb.com — we read everything.

Where should I start?

Start with an Intro class if you're new to reformer pilates — it's paced and cued for first-timers so you learn the FlowFormer setup and springs before anything else. From there, Signature, Yin, and Barre 45 are all open to you. Signature is all levels: our instructors give modifications and progressions throughout, and there's standing work built in.

I've never done pilates. Is this for me?

Yes — and especially yes. Reformer pilates is one of the most beginner-friendly strength practices because the machine supports you while it challenges you.

How fit do I need to be to start?

Not fit at all. We program for every body — including people coming back from injury, post-partum, or who haven't moved in years. Just tell your instructor at check-in.

Can I take class with an injury or after surgery?

We program for every body, including people coming back from injury, and modifications are always available. Always let your instructor know at the start of class about any injuries or recent surgeries — your safety is our priority. If you have a recent injury or a pre-existing medical condition, please check with your physician before starting a new exercise program.

Can I take class while pregnant or post-partum?

Our classes welcome every body, including post-partum. If you're pregnant, please check with your physician before starting a new exercise program, and always let your instructor know at the start of class so they can offer modifications throughout. Your safety is our priority.

Is Signature suitable for beginners?

Yes. Signature is an all-levels class — our instructors offer modifications and progressions throughout, and there's standing work built in, so you can meet the class wherever you are. We simply recommend taking an Intro class first so you're comfortable with the FlowFormer setup and springs before you flow.
