Your local movement sanctuary.

flowhouse is a coastal movement sanctuary blending reformer pilates, barre, and intentional strength — designed to feel equal parts grounding, elevated, and alive.

Our 50-minute reformer classes are sequenced for time under tension, continuous movement, and breath-led pacing — low-impact strength that supports the body long-term. Barre 45 rounds out the week with alignment, muscular endurance, and a full-body burn without high-impact stress.

the equipment

The FlowFormer.

Every reformer class is taught on the FlowFormer — our custom reformer and the foundation of the Flowhouse Method, developed specifically for the way we teach. The studio holds ten of them, with matte-black frames, the flowhouse wordmark on the side rail, and overhead cable straps for long-lever upper-body work.

Alongside them, two barre walls — solid wood barres on minimal black hardware. One is for our Barre 45 classes, with twenty-five spots; the other is for warmups and stretch.

New to the reformer? Start with an Intro class — paced and cued for first-time movers, the place to learn the reformer before anything else.

the experience

More than a workout.

Warm walnut feature walls, large sphere pendant lighting, and a calm coastal palette — designed to slow you down the moment you walk in. Every class closes with a slow stretch and cool down, so you walk out taller than you came in.

Then stay. Grab a juice, chat with the community, and linger in the calm. The hour on the FlowFormer is the start of the ritual — not the end of it.
